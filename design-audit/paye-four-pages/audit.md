# PAYE education pages — dumb-proof audit

Audited at 1440 × 1000 on 19 August 2026.

## Verdict

No. The eligible-deductions page is close, but the other three still ask users to process too much information before reaching a simple answer.

## 1. How PAYE is calculated

**Health:** Needs major simplification.

The page is well organised for a reference guide, but not for a beginner. The introduction, key rules, example, FAQs and source notes repeat several of the same concepts.

**Highest-impact change:** Lead with five plain calculation steps and one worked example. Move FAQs, manual-check guidance and detailed sources into collapsed sections.

## 2. Eligible deductions

**Health:** Mostly healthy.

This is the clearest of the four pages. The main remaining issue is duplication: the jump-link grid lists the same deductions that appear immediately afterward in the table.

**Highest-impact change:** Remove the jump-link grid. Start with “Use only amounts you can confirm. Leave anything else at ₦0,” followed directly by the deduction rows. On narrow screens, turn the wide table into stacked cards.

## 3. Tax bands

**Health:** Needs major simplification; weakest of the four.

The worked example and rate table are useful, but they are followed by several long explanation blocks. The narrow text column and large unused right side make the page feel longer and harder than it needs to be.

**Highest-impact change:** Keep one worked example, the rate table and two short callouts: “Taxable income is not always gross salary” and “A higher rate applies only to the part inside that band.” Collapse or move the remaining prose.

## 4. Gross salary vs take-home pay

**Health:** Needs major simplification.

The hero and “Direct answer” repeat the same definition. The page then repeats it again through calculation steps, deductions, job advice, FAQs and sources.

**Highest-impact change:** Use the title “Gross pay vs take-home pay,” show two side-by-side definitions, one formula, four steps and a short list of common deductions. Put job-offer advice, FAQs and sources behind optional disclosures.

## Cross-page consistency

- Give every guide the same structure: direct answer, simple example, optional detail, next action.
- Use one shared guide navigation. The gross-versus-net guide is currently missing from the guide trail shown on the other pages.
- Limit each page to one primary action and one secondary action.
- Avoid repeating introductions, definitions and reassurances within the same page.
- Replace long always-visible reference material with progressive disclosure.

## Accessibility risks to verify

- Convert wide tables into readable stacked content on mobile while preserving table semantics where appropriate.
- Check muted grey text contrast and the small uppercase labels.
- Keep heading order logical and give disclosure controls clear accessible names and states.
- Reduce unnecessary anchor links and tab stops.
- Verify keyboard focus, 200% zoom, screen-reader output and mobile reflow separately.

## Evidence limits

This is a screenshot-based visual and content audit at one desktop viewport. It does not verify interaction states, mobile layouts, keyboard behaviour, screen-reader semantics or the legal accuracy of the tax content.
