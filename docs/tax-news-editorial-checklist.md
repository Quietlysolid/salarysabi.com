# SalarySabi tax-news editorial checklist

Use this checklist before publishing or updating a tax explainer.

## 1. Classify the event

- **Announced:** A government official or agency described a proposal. It is not yet law.
- **Enacted:** The final legislation has been signed or formally published.
- **Guidance issued:** The responsible authority published implementation guidance.
- **Calculator updated:** SalarySabi changed and tested its ruleset against the final source.

Never describe an announcement or bill as an enacted rule.

## 2. Verify the primary source

- Link the official legislation, gazette, revenue-authority guidance or government statement.
- Record the relevant section, paragraph, appendix and page.
- Confirm publication date, commencement date and any transition period.
- Separate confirmed rules from open questions.
- Do not rely on a social post, newspaper summary or commentary as the sole source.

## 3. Answer the paycheck questions

- What changed?
- Who is affected?
- When does it start?
- What could change on a payslip or in take-home pay?
- Did the SalarySabi calculator change?
- Which ruleset contains the change?
- What remains unclear or unsupported?

## 4. Protect trust

- Name the author and link to the About page.
- State that SalarySabi is not personal tax advice.
- Do not imply accountant or tax-professional review unless it happened and is documented.
- Use short sentences and plain language. Keep pidgin to headlines or calls to action.
- Do not publish a calculation example until automated tests cover it.

## 5. Publish and distribute

- Add a unique title, description, canonical URL and Article structured data.
- Add accurate published and updated dates.
- Link to the calculator, methodology, changelog and relevant evergreen guide.
- Add the canonical URL to the sitemap with the true modification date.
- Share a short summary only after the article is live and checked.
- Request indexing in Google Search Console.

## 6. Correct quickly

- Update the article and modification date.
- Record calculator-impacting corrections in the tax changelog.
- Keep the previous rule and effective date visible when the change affects historical calculations.
- Route corrections to tax@salarysabi.com.
